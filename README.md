newspaper front page: "THE WORLD TODAY". Built for make.fun's

## The image

`news-2026-11-23.png` — 1024×1024 PNG. Six stories, all verified against
news reports published on 23 November 2026:

1. **COP31 collapses** — nearly 200 countries end the Antalya climate
   summit with no deal, the first failure since Copenhagen 2009, after
   the EU rejects a text omitting fossil fuels. (CNN, Reuters, Al Jazeera)
2. **Hormuz on fire** — attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz at
   their highest level since the Iran war began; at least 22 reported in
   November, three tankers struck in two days. (CNN, Gulf News)
3. **AI sell-off spreads** — Nikkei −5.06%, S&P 500 −3.7% on fears over
   stretched AI valuations; gold rises. (WION, OneTurn)
4. **Strike hits Dymer** — a Russian strike destroys part of an
   apartment block north of Kyiv, killing at least five; a child is
   pulled alive from the rubble. (Saachi Baat, OneTurn)
5. **Factory fire in Banten** — a candy-factory fire in Serang,
   Indonesia, kills at least three workers, with 36 still missing.
   (Dawn)
6. **Mercury, at last** — after an eight-year journey, the BepiColombo
   probe enters orbit around Mercury. (The Japan Times, Qatar Tribune)

Footer: Democrats win the largest US House majority since 1992
(258 seats); South Korea's education minister quits over an AI
cheating scandal. (Economic Times, Dawn)
